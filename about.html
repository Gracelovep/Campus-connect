Our story

Started in a dorm room

Campus Connect began in 2019 when a small group of students got tired of bouncing between the housing portal, the transit PDF, a club spreadsheet, and a resources page nobody could find. We built a single directory instead.

What started as a weekend project is now maintained by a student team working alongside Student Services, updated every semester with real listings, real routes, and real office hours.

Mission

One door, no dead ends

Every student should be able to find housing, get where they're going, find their people, and get help — without hunting across ten different sites to do it. We keep the directory current so you don't have to guess.
