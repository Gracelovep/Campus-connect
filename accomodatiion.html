How to apply

1

Submit your housing application

Opens each spring for the following year. Priority is given by submission date.

2

Rank your preferences

List up to 5 halls or listing types in order of preference.

3

Pay your deposit

A refundable deposit secures your assignment once offered.

4

Get your assignment

Assignments go out by early summer, with move-in details attached.
